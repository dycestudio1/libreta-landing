        FAQ

        What people ask us before getting started.

        Still have questions? Book a demo and we’ll go over them on the call.

        Do we have to enter customers and prices all over again?

        No. Libreta pulls customers, invoices, balances and price lists from your ERP and keeps them updated on its own. Reps never re-enter anything that already exists.

        Which systems does it work with?

        Today it connects directly to Doria. For Zureo, Odoo, SAP Business One and other systems, we build the connector during setup. Every plan includes the connection to your system.

        How does it know what to offer each customer?

        It looks at purchase history. If a customer buys something every two weeks and three have gone by, it’s a reorder. If 8 out of 10 customers who buy dish soap also buy bleach and this one doesn’t, it’s a cross-sell. If they stopped buying, it’s a win-back. If they have overdue invoices, it’s a collection. Every opportunity says why it shows up and how much it’s worth per month.

        Does the order go straight into my system?

        Yes. It goes into your system as an order, with the customer’s price list, ready to be invoiced. The supervisor sees it instantly in the dashboard.

        Can the assistant do anything without me knowing?

        No. Before creating a task, moving a visit or changing a route, it shows you exactly what it will do and waits for you to confirm. It only skips asking when it’s just looking up data.

        Do reps have to download an app?

        It opens on the phone, on Android or iPhone, and is saved to the home screen like any app. No app store needed.

        Does it track where reps are all day?

        No. It only logs arrival and departure at each visit, and only during the workday, between “Start” and “End.” We handle data under Uruguay’s Personal Data Protection Law 18.331 and help you inform your team.

        How long until it’s up and running?

        Two to four weeks, depending on your system and number of reps. That includes loading routes, targets and commissions, plus training for your field team.
